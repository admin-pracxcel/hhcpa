Patient Journey To begin peptide-based therapy, patients are required to

kidney).
● Mental health conditions, treatment history, and current emotional state.
● Sleep patterns and disturbances.
● Recent pathology results.
● Medical documents (e.g. imaging, referrals, specialist notes).
